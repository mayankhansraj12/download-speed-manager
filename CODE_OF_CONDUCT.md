# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity. To keep it that way, we
ask everyone who takes part in this project (in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) to:

- be kind and patient, and assume good intentions;
- give and take feedback about the work, not the person;
- accept that people disagree, and that the maintainers make the final call on what is merged;
- help newcomers find their way.

These are not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes,
sexual content, publishing someone's private information, and deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately, using the private
reporting option described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled in confidence.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people from the project, depending
on what happened. The aim is always to keep the project a place where people want to contribute.
